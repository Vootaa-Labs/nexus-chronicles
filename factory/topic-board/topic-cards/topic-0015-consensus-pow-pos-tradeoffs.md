## Why This Topic

PoW and PoS are widely repeated labels, but readers often mistake them for complete system explanations.

## Writing Angle

Separate consensus labels from real design dimensions such as finality, liveness, validator incentives, throughput pressure, and coordination cost.

## Risks

Do not oversimplify consensus into a two-column comparison chart.
