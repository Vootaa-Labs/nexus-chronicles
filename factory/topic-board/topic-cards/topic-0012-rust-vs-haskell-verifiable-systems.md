## Why This Topic

Many readers can feel the design tension between expressive correctness and operational rigor, but they rarely see it explained through language tradeoffs.

## Writing Angle

Use Rust and Haskell as two different lenses on safety, correctness, and system design rather than as a shallow language-war article.

## Risks

Do not reduce either language to stereotypes about speed versus elegance.
