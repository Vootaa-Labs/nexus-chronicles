## Why This Topic

Builder audiences increasingly hear post-quantum language, but they rarely get a grounded explanation of what changes in actual systems.

## Writing Angle

Focus on system pressure, migration cost, compatibility questions, and which parts of the stack feel the change first.

## Risks

Do not pretend post-quantum design is a single switch that upgrades the whole system.
