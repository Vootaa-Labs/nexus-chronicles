## Recommended Reading Choices / 阅读建议 / Sugerencias de lectura

1. Choose `manta-observer` if you want blockchain, AI, and agent-industry commentary.
2. 选择 `dolphin-explainer`，如果你想先读 RUST、共识、分片、并行架构等技术科普。
3. Elige `orca-auditor` si quieres leer sobre formal verification, post-quantum crypto y limites de seguridad.
4. Choose `nautilus-researcher` if you want slower, comparative essays such as Rust versus Haskell.
5. 选择 `jellyfish-curator`，如果你想顺着引用链和文章关系来读。
