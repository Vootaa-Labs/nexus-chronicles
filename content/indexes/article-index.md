## Archive Rules / 归档规则 / Reglas del archivo

1. Every article under `content/en/`, `content/zh/`, or `content/es/` must be linked from this page.
2. 每篇正式文章都必须在这个页面登记。
3. Todo articulo formal debe quedar registrado aqui.
4. Every article should also appear in its language-zone index.
5. Citation and response articles should also be reflected in [Citation Map](./citation-map.md).

## Entry Format / 条目格式 / Formato de entrada

Use one line per article:

```text
- [chronicle-0001 | Title](../en/sample-title.md) - stage: published - persona: octopus-architect - theme: execution-architecture
```
