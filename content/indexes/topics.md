## Industry Observation Column / 行业观察专栏 / Columna de observacion industrial

This lane follows the wider context around blockchain systems, AI infrastructure, and agent ecosystems.

这一专栏关注区块链系统、人工智能基础设施与智能体生态的外部环境和行业变化。

Esta columna sigue el contexto amplio de los sistemas blockchain, la infraestructura de IA y los ecosistemas de agentes.

Suggested themes:

1. blockchain market structure and protocol narratives
2. AI infrastructure shifts and coordination costs
3. agent tooling, control planes, and operational trust
4. what changes when AI systems begin to operate on-chain or around on-chain systems

## Technical Explainer Column / 技术科普专栏 / Columna de divulgacion tecnica

This lane turns difficult systems topics into readable essays without flattening the technical substance.

这一专栏把高门槛技术主题写成可读文章，但不牺牲技术实质。

Esta columna convierte temas complejos en ensayos legibles sin perder la sustancia tecnica.

Suggested themes:

1. Rust and Haskell for verifiable systems
2. formal verification in practice
3. post-quantum cryptography for builders
4. consensus design, including PoW and PoS tradeoffs
5. sharding systems and their coordination costs
6. parallel execution and architectural bottlenecks
